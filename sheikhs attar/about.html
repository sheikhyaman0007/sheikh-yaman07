Who are we?

Who could resist the irresistible charm of a fragrance that not only leaves an exquisite aura but also makes you the center of attraction?

Welcome to SHEIKH ATTAR, a fragrance house with over a decade of experience in crafting premium perfumes, attars, and bakhoor.

We specialize in a wide range of luxury fragrances designed to leave a lasting impression.

Vision:

To become India’s most loved fragrance brand, celebrating our rich heritage of attars and blending it with the charm of modern-day perfumes. We aim to take India’s timeless scents to the world, becoming a proud symbol of our country’s olfactory craftsmanship.

Mission:

We strive to offer every Indian a fragrance that feels personal, whether rooted in tradition or inspired by today’s trends. With a wide range of attars and perfumes, we’re here to make every moment memorable through scent, while upholding unmatched quality, authenticity, and heartfelt service.

SG Perfumes Pvt. Ltd offers luxurious, affordable attars and perfumes, focusing on high-quality, long-lasting, alcohol-free fragrances for all occasions.
